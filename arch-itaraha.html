Private Istaraha - Riyadh

Project Visuals

Project Overview

Private Istiraha (Rest House/Weekend Retreat) This project involved the design and development of a private Istiraha — a traditional Arabic rest house, typically used for weekend getaways and family gatherings. Located on the outskirts of Riyadh, the space was envisioned as a peaceful retreat, combining comfort with local cultural aesthetics.

My Role

My key responsibilities included:

  • Developing 3D visual concepts to help the client visualize the design.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, & Photoshop
