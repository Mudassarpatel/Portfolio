Project Overview

This project in Riyadh involved the complete interior design and execution of an art and sculpture gallery for Richard Orlinski, the renowned contemporary French artist. The design vision was to create an environment that not only showcases Orlinski’s iconic works but also enhances the visitor experience through a refined spatial narrative. Our scope included comprehensive interior design and detailed drawings, ensuring that every aspect of the gallery was meticulously planned to highlight the scale, form, and vibrancy of the sculptures. The interiors were conceived with a modern, minimalist approach, allowing the art pieces to remain the focal point, supported by carefully curated lighting, circulation flow, and material selection. The result is a gallery space that serves as a sophisticated platform for Orlinski’s creations, blending functionality with aesthetic restraint to celebrate art in its purest form.

My Role

I was actively involved in the design development and execution stages of the project.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Developing design concepts for key areas.
  • Creating mood boards and material selection boards to define the interior language and support client presentations.
  • Preparing a complete 2D drawing package, including general layouts, fit-out plans, and detailed joinery drawings to guide execution on-site.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop, MS Office
