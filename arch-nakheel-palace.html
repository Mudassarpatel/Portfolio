Project Overview

Al Nakheel Palace is a luxurious residential compound in Riyadh, reimagining the traditional Najdi palace for modern living. This 5,685 sqm sanctuary blends cultural heritage with luxury, creating a private oasis of elegant structures, shaded courtyards, and serene landscapes.

Design Concept

Inspired by the historic architecture of central Arabia, the design is a contemporary tribute to the Najdi style. The concept organizes the site around a series of inward-looking courtyards, providing privacy and promoting natural cooling. The layout creates a journey from formal arrival sequences to intimate, shaded outdoor rooms, all evoking the feeling of a verdant refuge in the desert.

Architectural Features

  • A Majestic Main Villa: The heart of the compound, designed with a commanding presence and organized around a central courtyard for privacy and natural cooling.
  • A Separate Guest Villa: A self-contained hospitality suite, offering visitor privacy while maintaining a cohesive architectural dialogue with the main villa.
  • Elegant Outdoor Seating Areas: A series of intimate, shaded spaces seamlessly integrated into the landscaping, creating perfect settings for entertainment and relaxation.
  • A Beautifully Crafted Gazebo: Serving as a focal point in the gardens, this structure provides a covered retreat for outdoor gatherings.
  • Service Buildings: Strategically positioned to ensure full functionality while maintaining the project's pristine aesthetic.
  • Well-Planned Landscaping: Lush palms and native vegetation frame the structures, transforming the grounds into a private oasis that enhances the overall living experience.

My Role

As the lead AutoCAD draftsman, I was responsible for translating the architectural vision into a comprehensive set of technical and presentation documents. My role was integral to every stage, from initial client approval to final construction.

Key Responsibilities & Programs Used:

  • Conceptual Design & Client Presentations: Produced compelling conceptual site plans, 3D massing models, and photorealistic visuals using AutoCAD, 3ds Max, and Photoshop to secure client approvals and effectively communicate the design intent.
  • Permit Acquisition & Compliance: Prepared and packaged complete sets of architectural drawings for official submission to the Riyadh municipal corporation, ensuring full compliance with local regulations using AutoCAD and MS Office for documentation.
  • Construction Documentation: Developed meticulously detailed final construction drawings, providing the precise instructions necessary for accurate site execution and ensuring the project's high-quality standards were met.

This project was a complete demonstration of my ability to leverage digital tools AutoCAD, 3ds Max, Photoshop, and MS Offic to manage the full drafting lifecycle, merging technical precision with strong visual presentation skills to deliver a high-end residential environment.
