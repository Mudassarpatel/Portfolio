Project Overview

This residential villa in Riyadh was transformed through a complete interior renovation that redefined its character while staying true to the client’s vision. The design concept blended modern, contemporary, and traditional elements, creating a harmonious balance between timeless elegance and modern-day comfort. Our scope involved a total turnaround of the existing interiors, reimagining each space to enhance functionality, aesthetics, and flow. The villa’s new look reflects a refined fusion—clean modern lines paired with warm traditional details and contemporary finishes, ensuring the home feels both inviting and sophisticated. From spatial planning and material selection to custom joinery and finishing details, every aspect was meticulously designed and executed to deliver a home that resonates with the client’s lifestyle and aspirations. The result is a villa that showcases a fresh, elegant, and personalized identity, transforming the original structure into a residence that truly feels like home.

My Role

I was actively involved in the design development and execution stages of the project.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Developing design concepts for key areas.
  • Creating mood boards and material selection boards to define the interior language and support client presentations.
  • Preparing a complete 2D drawing package, including general layouts, fit-out plans, and detailed joinery drawings to guide execution on-site.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop, MS Office
