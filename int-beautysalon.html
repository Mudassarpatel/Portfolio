Project Overview

This project is a luxury beauty salon and spa located in the heart of Riyadh, designed to embody elegance, comfort, and modern sophistication. The space integrates high-end interiors with functional layouts to provide clients a serene and indulgent experience. The design features open, airy spaces with high ceilings, neutral tones, and refined finishes that create a welcoming and relaxing atmosphere. Custom-designed workstations with large arched mirrors and sleek marble countertops offer both functionality and aesthetic appeal for hairstyling and beauty treatments. The salon’s seating is designed for comfort with plush, upholstered chairs in warm earthy tones paired with polished chrome bases, striking a balance between luxury and practicality. Strategic lighting enhances the overall ambiance, with modern suspended fixtures and spotlights that ensure a bright yet soothing environment. The spa section introduces a more intimate and tranquil setting, featuring semi-private curved partitions, soft natural palettes, and comfortable lounge-style seating for relaxation and treatments. The reception area follows a circular layout with a minimalist yet inviting design, providing an intuitive flow for guests as they enter the space. Overall, this salon and spa combines modern elegance, functional design, and a calming atmosphere, making it a premier destination in Riyadh for beauty, grooming, and relaxation services.
