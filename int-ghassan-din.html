Project Overview

This project involved the renovation of a private villa dining room in Riyadh, reimagining the space with a refined modern aesthetic while enhancing its functionality and ambiance. The design concept focused on creating an atmosphere of elegance and warmth, suitable for both intimate family gatherings and formal hosting. Carefully selected finishes, lighting, and furniture were integrated to elevate the space, while maintaining harmony with the villa’s existing design language. The result is a dining room that combines sophistication, comfort, and timeless appeal, transforming it into a central feature of the home.
