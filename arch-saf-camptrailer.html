Project Overview

This unique project involved the design and development of two custom-built camping trailers for a private client in Riyadh. The concept was to create “homes on wheels” that combine a rustic, antique exterior aesthetic with a luxurious and modern interior. The trailers were designed to be mobile yet fully functional living spaces, offering comfort and elegance while maintaining a rugged, vintage-inspired look from the outside.

My Role

I led the project's design development and documentation from concept to construction readiness.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Creating 3D visualizations to present interior concepts and design intent.
  • Selecting furniture and materials that aligned with the interior design theme.
  • Developing mood boards to define the overall style and ambiance.
  • Preparing a comprehensive 2D drawing package using AutoCAD for contractor execution, including plans, elevations, and technical details.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop, MS Office
