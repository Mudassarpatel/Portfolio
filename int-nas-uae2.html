Project Overview

This interior design project in Dubai was developed with a focus on achieving a modern and contemporary look, fully tailored to the client’s preferences. The design approach emphasized clean lines, refined finishes, and a sophisticated palette, creating spaces that are both stylish and comfortable. Every element, from spatial planning to material selection, was thoughtfully curated to reflect the client’s vision. The result is an interior that balances modern elegance with contemporary functionality, offering a home that is fresh, inviting, and truly personalized.

My Role

I was actively involved in the design development and execution stages of the project.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Developing design concepts for key areas.
  • Creating mood boards and material selection boards to define the interior language and support client presentations.
  • Preparing a complete 2D drawing package, including general layouts, fit-out plans, and detailed joinery drawings to guide execution on-site.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop, MS Office
