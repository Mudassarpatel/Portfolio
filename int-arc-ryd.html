Project Overview

This corporate office project was designed for Arcapita, a reputed global investment firm, with the aim of maintaining the brand’s established international design standards while incorporating subtle local elements. The concept focused on creating a professional, refined workspace that mirrors their global offices, using carefully selected materials and detailing to enhance the overall ambiance.

My Role

I was actively involved in the design development and execution stages of the project. My role ensured a seamless blend of Arcapita’s global identity with a cohesive and high-quality design solution.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Developing design concepts for key areas within the office.
  • Creating mood boards and material selection boards to define the interior language and support client presentations.
  • Preparing a complete 2D drawing package, including general layouts, fit-out plans, and detailed joinery drawings to guide execution on-site.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop, MS Office
