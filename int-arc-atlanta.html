Project Overview

This corporate office project was designed for Arcapita, a reputed global investment firm, with the objective of reflecting the brand’s signature design identity established at its Bahrain headquarters, while tailoring the space to suit its Atlanta, USA location. The design approach emphasized a modern yet timeless aesthetic, maintaining the brand’s international standards and professional character. By incorporating key design cues from the original Bahrain head office, the office resonates with Arcapita’s global identity, while introducing subtle adaptations that respond to the local context. The use of carefully selected materials, refined detailing, and a clean modern palette ensures a professional, sophisticated workspace that is both functional and aligned with the company’s international image.

My Role

I was actively involved in the design development and execution stages of the project. My role ensured a seamless blend of Arcapita’s global identity with a cohesive and high-quality design solution.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Developing design concepts for key areas within the office.
  • Creating mood boards and material selection boards to define the interior language and support client presentations.
  • Preparing a complete 2D drawing package, including general layouts, fit-out plans, and detailed joinery drawings to guide execution on-site.

Software Used: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop, MS Office
