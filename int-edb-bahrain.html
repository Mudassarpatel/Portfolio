Project Overview

The Economic Development Board – Bahrain project is located in the prestigious Arcapita Building, Manama, Bahrain. Designed with a Modern Contemporary approach, the project reflects sophistication, functionality, and a forward-looking vision aligned with the organization’s dynamic role in Bahrain’s growth. Our scope of work encompassed complete interior fit-out and detailed joinery works, ensuring the seamless integration of design and craftsmanship. The interior concept emphasizes clean lines, minimalistic yet elegant finishes, and the use of high-quality materials to create an inspiring and professional environment. Special attention was given to bespoke joinery details, enhancing both aesthetics and functionality. Each space has been carefully designed to foster collaboration, comfort, and efficiency while maintaining a refined corporate identity. The project successfully combines modern design principles with practical spatial planning, resulting in a contemporary workplace environment that supports innovation, productivity, and Bahrain’s commitment to economic progress.

My Role

I contributed to the project by providing detailed design support.

My key responsibilities included:

  • Creating high-quality 3D visualizations to help the client visualize the proposed design concepts.
  • Developing precise AutoCAD drawings such as general layouts, Fit-out and joinery details, including custom furniture, wall panels, and cabinetry.
